## What we do

Netvar Studio builds professional websites for *Handwerker* — electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, dental practices, and other local service businesses across Germany.

Most tradespeople in Germany have one of two problems: no website at all, or a site that hasn't been touched since 2015 and drives exactly zero leads. We fix that.

## What a Netvar site includes

- Clean, fast, mobile-first design
- Google-ready from day one (on-page SEO, structured data)
- Clear call-to-action: phone, WhatsApp, contact form
- DSGVO-compliant (Impressum, Datenschutz, cookie handling)
- Delivered in 7 days, no agency back-and-forth

## Built for this market

Germany has ~900,000 registered Handwerk businesses. The vast majority are underserved digitally — no Google presence, no clear online contact path, no way for new customers to find them. That's the gap Netvar fills.

We work exclusively with this segment. That focus means faster delivery, lower cost, and sites that actually match how German tradespeople and their customers think.
